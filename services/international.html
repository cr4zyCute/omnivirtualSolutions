Book Exhibit Plus – International

The Book Exhibit Plus – International service is customized to help you pursue international coverage and includes exhibits for your book at five major book events around the world. At each event, your book will be available to notable publishing industry personnel and potential readers.

Included in this service:

Display space for your title at five international book events

  • Frankfurt Book Fair - The largest book and media fair in the world gathers roughly 7,500 exhibitors from more than 100 countries in Frankfurt, Germany and provides virtually endless opportunities for your book.
  • The London Book Fair - This event gathers more than 24,000 attendees from 124 countries, giving your book a prime opportunity to be showcased in front of publishers, retailers, librarians, authors, readers and more.
  • US Book Show - Booksellers, librarians, readers, and publishers converge at PW's (Publishers Weekly) US Book Show to network with their fellow professionals, and look for information about upcoming titles. The US Book Show is an online festival that enables its attendees to hear top speakers from the book industry, and discover new books through an online exhibit from various publishers.
  • Guadalajara International Book Fair - Showcase your book at the largest meeting of the Spanish-language publishing industry. Over nine days, Guadalajara demonstrates the essence of the book industry, as book enthusiasts congregate to experience one of the most renowned international literary festivals.
  • Beijing International Book Fair - As the largest publishing industry event in the Asian market, the 2013 fair boasted more than 2,000 exhibitors from 76 countries. The sheer size of this event and market gives your book a great opportunity to be seen by booklovers.

Please note: If you are unable to exhibit your titles on the scheduled dates for any or all events, you can postpone your inclusion until the following year’s event(s).

How It Works:

  • At each show, your book will be assigned a number, either based on its Dewey Decimal subject category at library shows, or based on where it lies alphabetically by publisher at international venues.
  • Your book will be displayed along with other titles in a prime position on the exhibit floor.
  • Your book will be displayed face-out on the shelf.
  • A sticker bearing a unique QR code will be placed on the lower left hand side of the cover of the books that will be displayed. Book fair attendees can then scan the code using the CBE Book Fair Buddy app to learn more about your book. Using the app, they can add your book to their to-be-read list, share that with friends, family, or on their social media pages and even get connected to a variety of reseller sites where they can purchase your book.

Please note that the event organizers may change the schedule and book exhibit format (virtual, physical, or a combination of both) without prior notice. Please contact Omni to confirm dates for you.
