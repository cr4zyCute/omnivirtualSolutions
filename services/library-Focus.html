Library Focus

Libraries are beloved sites of community, history, and education. They’ve always been important to readers, and they continue to be essential today, even with digital media and publishing becoming more and more part of our everyday lives. It’s only natural that authors such as yourself strive to make your work available to the community by getting libraries to stock copies of your book. Unfortunately, convincing libraries to stock self-published titles can be challenging even for the savviest authors.

The Omni Library Focus Service helps you with your goal of making your book available in libraries through a strategic combination of print ads in two publications popular with librarians and a customized sales kit.

The Library Focus Service includes

  • Your book, pitched to the Collection Development Departments and Acquisition Librarians of 25 public libraries across the US (with focus on a specific area or state), over the span of three months, using a customized sales kit
  • A single-slot print ad in Baker and Taylor’s Forecast Catalog
  • A single-slot print ad in the quarterly publication ForeWord Magazine
  • The Booksellers Return Program for 12 months
  • A guarantee that at least 2 of the libraries will agree to test your book

About Forecast Catalog

The most widely circulated monthly publication of Baker & Taylor, Forecast Catalog boasts a subscriber base of 45,000 librarians in the US. It primarily promotes new and forthcoming adult hardcover and trade and mass-market paperback. This magazine enables publishers to reach out to libraries. By getting to know future bestsellers and other noteworthy titles, booksellers can make well-informed buying decisions. Your title information will be featured on a separate catalogue-style ad mailed with Forecast Magazine along with a select group of Author Solutions (Omni's parent company) authors.

About ForeWord Magazine

One of the most reputable publications in the industry, ForeWord is a bimonthly magazine with a readership of more than 30,000 librarians and booksellers— including 1,100 American Bookseller Association members—all of whom make buying decisions and reader recommendations that affect the reading habits of millions of patrons and consumers. It is carried in all Books-A-Million bookstores, select Barnes & Noble stores, and independent bookstores across the US. Founded in 1998, it is still the only magazine showcasing critical reviews and title trends exclusively from the expanding independent publishing market.
