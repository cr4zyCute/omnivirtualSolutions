Book Exhibit Plus – National

If you’re having trouble finding the right avenues to gain exposure for yourself and your book, our Book Exhibit Plus – National service should not be overlooked. By exhibiting your book at major industry events within the U.S., you will have the opportunity to connect with the publishing community at large.

Included in this service:

Display space for your title at four national book events

  • LibLearnX (formerly ALA Midwinter) - Publishers, retailers, librarians, authors, readers, speakers and more all come together at the yearly ALA Midwinter Meeting. Your book will have ample opportunities to be promoted among industry enthusiasts.
  • Public Library Association Conference OR Association of College and Research Libraries Conference*
    • Public Library Association Conference - Public library professionals from cities across the country will have the opportunity to discover your book.
    • Association of College and Research Libraries Conference - The Association of College and Research Libraries Conference will be held in Portland, Oregon. ACRL is a division of the American Library Association, assuring that your title will get exposure to book lovers and experts.
  • ALA Annual Conference and Exhibition – The ALA’s Annual Conference boasts big-name speakers, a booming crowd of booklovers and, with the Book Exhibit Plus – National service, your book.
  • National Council Teachers of English Convention and Exhibition – Gain access to thousands of literacy educators who lead the teaching of the English language and influence purchasing decisions across a wide range of grade levels in this century-old gathering.

Please note: If you are unable to exhibit your titles on the scheduled dates for any or all events, you can postpone your inclusion until the following year’s event.

How It Works:

  • At each show, your book will be assigned a number, either based on its Dewey Decimal subject category at library shows, or based on where it lies alphabetically by publisher at international venues.
  • Your book will be displayed along with other titles in a prime position on the exhibit floor.
  • Your book will be displayed face-out on the shelf.
  • A sticker bearing a unique QR code will be placed on the lower left hand side of the cover of the books that will be displayed. Book fair attendees can then scan the code using the CBE Book Fair Buddy app to learn more about your book. Using the app, they can add your book to their to-be-read list, share that with friends, family, or on their social media pages and even get connected to a variety of reseller sites where they can purchase your book.

*The following shows are biennial—occurring once every two years. Depending on when you purchase the service, your book will be registered to the next available show.

Please note that the event organizers may change the schedule and book exhibit format (virtual, physical, or a combination of both) without prior notice. Please contact Omni to confirm dates for you.
