Ingram Supplement Marketing

Even with the Internet, it can be quite difficult for most readers to find their next favorite book. But for Ingram’s subscribers, fresh stories aren’t really hard to come by. They know that they can always expect interesting titles from its trusted source: Ingram Advance Catalog Supplements.

Since 2009, Ingram has been at the forefront of the publishing industry, and has a history of comprehensive trade catalogs. Its Ingram Advance Catalog covers specific interests, themes and genres, and is sent to more than 9,000 retailers and librarians every month—as well as the 47,000+ recipients of Ingram’s monthly e-catalog.

Ingram delivers supplements directly to its subscribers, either alongside the Advance Catalog or as a standalone offering. Through our Ingram Supplement Marketing service, your book will be prominently featured alongside other titles in the same genre or theme, ensuring it reaches the right audience. Each supplement page shows up to four titles, maximizing visibility.
